UI Design

A collection of the main game interfaces, showcasing onboarding, the home screen, level progression, word puzzles, rewards and more.

Onboarding
Screen

MeowWords welcomes players with a charming splash screen, leads them into a scenic level journey, and introduces the word-search gameplay through an interactive puzzle.

The puzzle screen captures the core MeowWords experience, challenging players to find hidden words using the given word list. The clear layout, highlighted discoveries, and handy hints make puzzles intuitive and rewarding, encouraging players to keep progressing.

Special Puzzle

The special puzzle appears before players reach a milestone, adding a fun challenge to their journey. Players uncover hidden words beneath cat icons, collecting each cat as its word is found. Hints help reveal hidden letters, and collecting all the cats completes the level, rewarding players with a refreshing break from regular gameplay.

Puzzle
Completion

Celebrates players for finding hidden words and collecting curious cats, while showing their reward progress. The cheerful animation and milestone rewards create a sense of achievement, encouraging players to solve more puzzles and earn exciting rewards.

Exit
Confirmation

Appears when players attempt to leave a puzzle, warning them that quitting may cost a life. The expressive cat character adds an emotional touch, while the clear Resume and Quit buttons let players choose whether to continue playing or exit, creating a more informed experience.
Outro

No Lives Left

Appears when players run out of lives, offering a quick way to recover and return to the puzzle. The free life timer and No Stop Bundle provide recovery options, helping players choose between waiting and purchasing resources.

Outro Screen Stats

The stats screen displays the player’s progress through cats collected, puzzles solved, and milestones reached. It helps players track their achievements, understand their progress, and stay motivated to reach the next goal.
Offers bundles of 3, 5, or 9 lives at different price points. The clear pricing and visual reward presentation help players compare options easily and continue their gameplay with fewer interruptions.
The milestone screen celebrates players’ progress by unlocking a special badge and revealing a fun fact about the cute cat character. The surprise reward, glowing presentation, and bonus lives and hints make each milestone feel rewarding, adding personality and encouraging players to continue their journey.
