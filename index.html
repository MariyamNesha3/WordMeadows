Quest Center

The Quest feature has two sections:

  • Daily Puzzle: Includes 7 tasks linked to monthly milestones—1 fixed task and 2 tasks repeated 3 times each. Users must complete at least 1 daily task to make monthly progress.
  • Achievements: Contains ongoing tasks that users can complete to earn rewards.
The Leaderboard showcases top players based on their weekly scores, highlighting the top three players and their rankings. Users can track their position, compare scores, and stay motivated to play more and climb the leaderboard.
The mini leaderboard appears on the puzzle outro, showcasing the top 4 players and their weekly scores. It gives users a quick performance comparison and motivates them to improve their ranking by playing more puzzles.
Leaderboard

Collectible Event

The Collectible Event allows users to collect special items placed on specific letters in the puzzle. When users complete a word containing a collectible, they automatically collect that item and progress toward milestone rewards.
